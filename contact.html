Good to Know

Common Questions

How long does a detail take?

Most exterior or interior details take 2 to 4 hours. A full detail can take most of a day. Paint correction and ceramic coating may take longer depending on the vehicle.

Do you come to me?

Yes. We're fully mobile. We detail your vehicle at your home, apartment, or workplace in Killeen and nearby areas.

What do I need to do before you arrive?

Remove personal items you want to keep safe, and make sure we have room to work around the vehicle. If your apartment or workplace has parking rules, let us know when you book.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on vehicle size and condition. Send us a request and we'll give you a clear quote before any work starts.

Do you work on trucks and SUVs?

Yes. Cars, trucks, SUVs, and everything in between.
