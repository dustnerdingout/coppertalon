Good to Know

Common Questions

How long does a detail take?

A Maintenance Wash takes about 1 hour. Exterior and Interior Details take about 2 hours, Clay & Seal about 2.5 hours, and a Full Detail about 3.5 hours. Packages run 3.5 to 6 hours.

Do you come to me?

Yes. We're fully mobile. We detail your vehicle at your home, apartment, or workplace in Killeen, Harker Heights, Copperas Cove, Nolanville, Belton, Temple, Lampasas, and nearby towns.

What do I need to do before you arrive?

Remove personal items you want to keep safe, and make sure we have room to work around the vehicle. If your apartment or workplace has parking rules, let us know when you book.

How much does it cost?

Services start at $79 for a Maintenance Wash. Pricing depends on vehicle size: Sedan / Coupe, SUV / Crossover, or Truck / Van / 3 Row. See our pricing page for every service. All services are taxable.

Do I need a deposit?

A deposit holds your spot when you book: $25 for most services, $50 for the Showroom Package, and none for the Mobile Maintenance Wash.

Do you work on trucks and SUVs?

Yes. Cars, trucks, SUVs, and everything in between.
